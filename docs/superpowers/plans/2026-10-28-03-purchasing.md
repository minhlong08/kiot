# Milestone 3 — Suppliers & Purchasing Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Suppliers with derived payable debt, purchase orders (Đặt hàng nhập), goods receipts that raise stock and recompute weighted-average cost per branch, purchase returns, incoming invoices, service purchases, supplier payments — every money movement recorded in a partner ledger and (for cash paid/received) a cash-book voucher.

**Architecture:** Two new single-writer services: `partnerLedger.post()` (supplier/customer debt, also used by M4/M5) and `cash.record()` (cash vouchers, the core of the M6 cash book, built now so purchasing never needs a retrofit). Receipts/returns/POs follow the M2 document pattern (`docCode`, `withProductInfo`, draft → completed → cancelled with reversing entries). Admin editors reuse `DocumentLinesEditor` with unit support.

**Tech Stack:** as M2.

**Spec:** design §6 (Nhập hàng, Nhà cung cấp), §10 (Đặt hàng nhập), requirements §3, §7 (auto entries), data-model rules.

## Global Constraints

- All M1/M2 constraints.
- Supplier debt = `Supplier.debt` cached balance, written **only** by `postPartnerLedger`; `GET /api/suppliers/:id/recompute` (admin, tests) recomputes from the ledger and must match.
- Sign convention: `PartnerLedger.amount > 0` means the shop owes the supplier more (receipt), `< 0` owes less (payment, return). For customers (M4+): `> 0` customer owes the shop more.
- Cash vouchers: `CashVoucher.kind` `in|out`, amount always positive; auto vouchers carry `sourceType/sourceId/sourceCode` and are cancelled (status `cancelled`) when their source is cancelled — never deleted.
- Code prefixes (add to settings `codePrefixes`, change defaults): `purchaseOrder 'PDN'`, `purchaseReceipt 'PN'`, `purchaseReturn 'THN'`, `servicePurchase 'MDV'`, `supplier 'NCC'`, manual `cashIn 'PT'`, `cashOut 'PC'`, auto receipt payment `'TTPN'`, supplier debt payment `'TTNCC'`, purchase-return refund `'TTTHN'`, service purchase payment `'TTMDV'`.
- Receipt unit cost per base unit = `round((lineNet − orderDiscountShare + otherCostShare) / (qty × conversion))`, shares allocated proportionally to line net (`qty × unitCost − lineDiscount`), rounding remainder on the last line so shares sum exactly.
- `otherCosts` on a receipt are payable to the supplier (part of `total`) — ruling: no separate "other payee" costs.

## Review Focus

1. **Cancelling a receipt whose goods were partly sold** → reversal may make stock negative; refused with `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` unless negative stock is allowed (Task 5).
2. **Returning more than was received on the linked receipt (across several returns)** → refused `RETURN_EXCEEDS_RECEIPT` (Task 6).
3. **Paying a supplier more than the debt** → allowed, debt goes negative (supplier owes the shop) and shows as such; not silently capped (Task 3).
4. **Receipt lines in a non-base unit (hộp = 6 cái)** → stock +6 per hộp and cost per cái = unit cost / 6 (Task 5).
5. **Completing a receipt from a PO twice / receiving more than ordered** → PO becomes `completed`, extra quantity allowed but PO `incoming` never goes below 0 (Task 4/5).
